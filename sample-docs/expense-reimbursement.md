# Expense Reimbursement

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in unusual circumstances approved by finance.

Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Meals during business travel are capped at $75/day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstance.

Reimbursements are paid out with the next payroll cycle after approval, typically within 10 business days.
