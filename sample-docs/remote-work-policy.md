# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Requests for full-time remote work require manager approval and must be renewed every 6 months.

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10am-4pm in their local timezone. The company does not reimburse home internet or coworking space costs.

Equipment (laptop, monitor, keyboard) is provided by the company and must be returned within 14 days of employment ending.
