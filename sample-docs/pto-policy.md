# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year, front-loaded at the start of each calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; anything beyond that is forfeited.

PTO requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for anything longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not require advance notice.

Unused PTO is not paid out when an employee leaves the company, except where required by local law.
