# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Day 1: IT sets up your laptop and accounts. HR walks you through benefits enrollment. You'll be assigned an onboarding buddy for your first 30 days.

Week 1: Complete the mandatory security training and sign the code of conduct acknowledgment. Meet with your manager to set 30/60/90 day goals.

Month 1: First 1:1 with your skip-level manager. Onboarding buddy relationship formally ends after 30 days, but you can keep in touch informally.
